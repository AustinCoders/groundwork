# Benchmark, October 2026: is Groundwork's content as good as the big players'?

Read on 2026-10-21 by five independent agents, each told to be skeptical and to separate what they observed from what they inferred. Public pages only. LeetCode, Glassdoor, Naukri Code 360 and AmbitionBox return 403, so scores for them are inferred from search snippets and are lower confidence. The method brief is in `method.md`.

## Verdict

Groundwork is not yet better than the big players overall. Per page it is accurate, current and honest, and in places ahead. As a catalogue, and on trust and scale, it is behind.

| Area                              | Verdict                                                      | Evidence                                                                                                                           | Report                 |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| JavaScript, React, interview book | Ahead on accuracy and freshness, behind on depth and breadth | All 261 reference solutions pass 2,098 tests; React chapters average 862 prose words against a 3,500 to 5,000 standard; no quizzes | `frontend.md`          |
| DSA                               | Ahead on written depth, behind on scale and language reach   | 694 DSA exercises; 143 of NeetCode 150 covered; 73 of 281 briefs with a stated complexity have a large-input test                  | `dsa.md`               |
| System design and LLD             | Best per page, middle as a catalogue                         | Maths and outside claims re-derived and correct; no AI system design; chat, video and wallet designs thin                          | `system-design.md`     |
| Company data                      | Best on curation and honesty, behind on volume and recency   | 357 source URLs; 81 of 130 answers rest on one source; 54 answer-source links carry no year                                        | `company-questions.md` |
| Product and trust                 | Fast and accessible, weak on trust signals                   | Live site matches `main`; "handwritten" contradicts the documented agent pipeline; 25 of 30 topics are empty                       | `live-site.md`         |

## Where Groundwork is genuinely ahead

Accuracy of what is written, runnable practice with tests, evidence labels and gap notes on company data, freshness, no paywall, page speed and accessibility.

## Hard truths

- 358 of 593 chapter slots are empty. HTML, CSS, Node, SQL, Python, Java and others have nothing yet.
- One author and no external reviewer, and chapters show no author, date or sources even though the data exists.
- The "handwritten" claim, the "955 problems" figure (694 are DSA), "420+ questions answered" (194 are one-line bullets) and "every idea is followed by an exercise" are not literally true.
- Volume cannot be matched by one author. The credible position is smaller, cleaner, honest, with solutions.

## Recommended order

1. Phase A, truth and trust (about 1 week, 1M to 2M tokens): fix the false claims, show author, review date and sources on every chapter, add about, changelog and contact pages, fix the 3 broken links, the 6 JS defects and the 22 DSA audit bugs, re-grade the inflated company answers, add "last verified" dates.
2. Phase B, make what exists top notch (4 to 6 weeks, 15M to 25M tokens): quizzes for JS and React, written answers for the 613 interview follow-ups, solution write-ups and large-input tests for the exercises, deeper React chapters, AI system design and the missing designs, an LLD teaching layer, 2025 and 2026 company reports.
3. Phase C, fill the empty topics (months, 30M tokens and up): only when the "whole map" claim should become true. Until then claim only what exists.
4. Do not compete on volume with NeetCode or LeetCode, and do not add Vue, Angular or Svelte tracks.

Each report ends with its own prioritised gap list (11 to 19 items) with effort and token class.

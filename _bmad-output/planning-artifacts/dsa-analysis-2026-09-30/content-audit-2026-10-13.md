# DSA content audit, 13 October 2026

Five read-only reviewers read all 34 written chapters against their `practice` lists, the syllabus in `content/topics.ts` and the Blind75, NeetCode150 and Grokking-style curricula. This file is the brief the writing agents work from.

## Verdict

The 34 written chapters are accurate and well explained where they teach. The gap is breadth: most chapters teach one or two flagship problems while the `practice` list names ten to twenty. Eight chapters the syllabus promises are not written. Tries, topological patterns, monotonic stack, complexity analysis, interview strategy and segment/Fenwick trees are close to KEEP. Everything else needs sections added.

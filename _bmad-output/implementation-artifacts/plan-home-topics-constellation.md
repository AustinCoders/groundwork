## Intent

**Problem:** The home Topics section (`#shelf`) does not please the owner: the right stage is six tilted cards, each holding meaningless grey placeholder bars that look like an unfinished skeleton, and the left side is a plain category list. It is the section where a visitor chooses what to read, and it neither shows the breadth of the platform (32 topics in 8 categories) nor feels alive.

**Approach:** Replace the stage with a hand-drawn CONSTELLATION MAP: each topic is a star/node, grouped by category into clusters (Languages, Web, Backend and APIs, Data, Computer science, DevOps and cloud, Engineering practice, AI), with thin constellation lines between related topics (JavaScript to React, React to Next.js, DSA to the interview book...), so the whole platform reads as one connected map. Written topics are large and lit with a label and chapter chip; coming-soon topics are small and dim. Hovering or focusing a node pops a taped topic card and lights the node's connections. The left category tablist highlights and zooms a cluster. Everything is built from real data and real links.

## Implementation Notes

Progress log (restart-safe). Scratch copy: `<scratchpad>/cst` (sync with `sync-cst.sh`, production build with `rebuild.sh`, served on 3101, screenshots with `cshot.mjs`, e2e on 3100). **What is built**
- Right side: a METRO MAP. `lib/metro.ts` (pure, tested): eight category lines (Languages, Web, Backend and APIs, Data, Computer science, DevOps and cloud, Engineering practice, AI), each a hand-composed polyline of horizontal, vertical and 45 degree legs with rounded corners (24 units), a line badge at the start and a terminus bar at the end, on a 700 by 680 reference canvas. Topics are stations at even arc-length spacing along their category's line (explicit ordered lists, written topics first). Four interchanges (JavaScript on Languages and Web; TypeScript on Languages and Backend; SQL and Databases on Data and Backend; Docker on DevOps and Backend) are topics listed on two lines; the second line's start offset is derived so both lines pass through the station (the unit test asserts it within 1.5 units). A topic or category missing from the hand data is appended to the end of its line (the line is extended, turning at the canvas edge), unknown categories go on the AI line; label overlaps in that case fall back to a free-space scan, so adding a topic never overlaps. The Interview book is a station on the Computer science line.
- Labels sit perpendicular to the line direction on alternating sides (a pure helper tries sides, slides and offsets and checks boxes, discs, badges, decor, the dock and sampled line points); tests assert no label, station, badge or line collision.
- Cards: no free 224 by 124 window exists next to most stations on a map this dense, so every card opens in a reserved DOCK (a clear block right of the Backend line, kept free of lines and labels by the tests) or in the nearest free window the layout finds, with a hand-drawn dashed leader arrow from the card to its station. Cards never cover another label, station or badge (tested for all 31 stations at three desktop sizes in the browser too).
- Train: eight decorative SVG trains (one per line, `offset-path` of the line, `offset-distance` from `--stop`); the selected line's train is shown and glides (CSS transition, 1.5 s) to just before its first written station, "Ready now" parks it at JavaScript, under reduced motion it is parked with no transition and no bob. No per-frame React state.
- Motion with the scene system: lines draw in from their badges (`edge` kind, scrubbed by the scroll, reversible, at rest on anchor landing), stations and badges pop in in sequence (`star` kind, scale and opacity only), twinkle is opacity-only CSS behind `[data-live]`. Per the owner, NO pointer parallax on this scene: `Stage` has a `pointer` prop (false here) so `useStage` attaches no listener, no rAF loop and no data-in observer; the pose helpers lost their pointer terms (`starPose(out, lag, scale, enter, exit)`), the `layer` fx kind and the marker hover scale were deleted; an e2e moves the pointer over the whole map and asserts no element's box or transform changes. Other scenes' parallax is untouched.
- Left column (accepted): `TopicsLegend.tsx` and `legend.module.css`: find-a-topic search (labelled input, clear button, status line, Enter on one match), the legend rows (a glyph of the real line shape in the category colour, "x of y written", a progress line, the open row expanding into topic chips that are real links and light their station; hovering a row previews its line), a Start here card, Browse all topics. Below 1081px the same DOM reflows into the compact tabs and card list (search stays; no canvas).
- Decor: sticker, note ("pick a stop"), three real-data chips (420+ questions, 27 rounds, 538 exercises) in the dock block, a key (filled, hollow, double ring) in a margin; all inside the canvas, away from lines and labels (unit-tested).

**Known limits and deviations**: the real data has 30 shelf topics plus the Interview book (31 stations, the plan said 32); only 5 topics are written; labels and cards are laid out for the current set (a topic that becomes written changes marker and label size and is re-placed by a free-space scan if it no longer fits, which can put its label away from its station); under 1081px there is no canvas; the train bob is a time-based idle animation (off under reduced motion), the only motion on the map apart from the scroll entry and the twinkle.

**Review patch round (blind-hunter and edge-case-hunter)**: `lib/metro.ts` now samples paths with cumulative lengths (binary search), drops zero-length legs, extends lines without NaN or stacked stations and reports `overflow`, drops empty lines, resolves interchange offsets in passes, slots new topics after the last present listed topic, widens the label search then a raster scan and marks a label that still cannot fit as `bare` (name shown in the card only), rounds emitted numbers, memoises by input signature and is stable under shuffled input; unknown categories get their own grey "More" line. Measured `layoutMetro`: Chromium 10.5 ms cold and 3.6 ms warm, Node 12.8 ms cold and 5 ms warm (cards use a precomputed window grid). Search normalises names (node js, c plus plus), knows aliases, opens the exact name on Enter, ArrowDown enters the results, shows an empty state and says "Search cleared."; choosing a category clears the query; Escape works from a legend chip; the train follows the engaged, previewed or searched line and hides without `offset-path` support; `useStage` always arms the in-view marker and only the pointer part is optional; station links describe themselves with the tagline and facts only.
